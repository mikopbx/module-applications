## Как подписать / How to sign

Every author of the commits in an application pull request posts a comment with only this line:

    I have read the MikoPBX Publisher Agreement and I hereby sign it

The `Publisher agreement` workflow records the signature (GitHub login, id, pull request, time) in the branch
`publisher-agreement-signatures`, and refuses a signature from someone who is not an author of a commit in the pull
request. Whether a comment is enough, or a signature in the cabinet is needed as well, is decided by MIKO.
